# Clothing Store Data Analysis (Excel)

An Excel-based sales analysis of an online clothing store. I used pivot tables and charts to understand sales, orders, customers and sales channels, and put everything together in one annual report dashboard.

## Dataset

- File: `Store_Data_Analysis.xlsx`
- Records: 31,047 orders
- Main columns: Order ID, Gender, Age Group, Date, Status, Channel, Category, Size, Qty, Amount (INR), Ship State

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Dashboard design (Annual Report sheet)

## What I Analysed

- Monthly sales and number of orders
- Men vs Women sales
- Order status (Delivered, Cancelled, Returned, Refunded)
- Top 10 states by sales
- Sales share by channel (Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, etc.)
- Age group split by gender

## Key Insights

- Total sales: about INR 21.18 million from 31,047 orders (average order value about INR 682)
- Women bring around 64% of sales, men around 36%
- Amazon is the biggest channel (about 36%), followed by Myntra (23%) and Flipkart (22%)
- Maharashtra and Karnataka are the top two states by sales
- About 92% of orders are delivered; around 8% are cancelled, returned or refunded
- Sales are highest in March and drop gradually towards November
- "Set" and "Kurta" are the best-selling categories
